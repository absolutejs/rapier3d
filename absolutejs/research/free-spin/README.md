# Force-free spin precision investigation

The probe covers 336 cases across short/long boxes and spheres, identity/tilted initial poses, principal-axis spins at 6/30rad/s, an oblique spin, 60/960Hz and 1/4/32/128 solver subdivisions. Every step is evaluated; peak and final witnesses are retained. Principal-axis and isotropic motion have analytic references. Asymmetric oblique rotation uses Euler equations integrated in f64 with RK4; two time resolutions, momentum and energy conservation independently check the reference.

Published stable 0.6.0 and private candidate 21 produce identical measurements in all 336 cases. No case approaches its angular speed cap. The largest energy error is about 0.4033% over 0.1s in a long box spinning about a principal axis at 960Hz/128 subdivisions. A true principal-axis free spin has constant world angular velocity. More subdivisions can therefore amplify roundoff even while reducing time-discretization error. Low-subdivision orientation/precession errors are measured separately.

Candidate 22 applies only the computed gyroscopic momentum increment instead of reconstructing the entire angular velocity. Genuine precession remains enabled. Across 336 cases the maximum relative energy error falls from 0.40327% to 0.05620%; sphere error is at most 1.96e-15. One tilted long-box case regresses by 1.305 ppm. No angular-velocity error regresses by more than 0.0001 rad/s. Remaining errors include finite-precision conditioning and time discretization.

The 144 private tagged contact cases retain maximum penetration of about 2.002 mm, with no depth regression over one micrometer. Maximum relative contact energy gain falls from 0.00095817 to 0.00000076034. This does not qualify the private contact solver for publication. Its suite remains 21 passing / 4 failing tests: the four upstream-motion parity controls fail, now also because the intended gyro correction changes dynamic motion. All results, including failures, are retained.

The initial short CPU sample was noisy and is preserved. `warm-cost-probe.ts` adds global warmup and five alternating paired samples of 512 steps, bounded per sample and overall. Candidate/baseline median ratios range from 0.888 to 1.063 across six configurations, with overlapping timing ranges in the slower case. These are CPU-only microbenchmarks, not browser FPS or capacity claims.

The stable release work isolates the gyro increment only; it does not copy the private tagged contact or CCD experiments. Stable qualification must retain explicit old/new motion measurements rather than require exact agreement with the old rounding behavior.
